## Project Idea

- Initial idea: The strongest direction is an asymmetric first-person 3D facility escape with two physical infiltrators. The compact opening has roughly three connected spaces: two short enemy encounters followed by a cooperative bomb gate where one infiltrator plants the charge and the other triggers it. They then reach a getaway car; during a short authored chase along one path with roughly two directional turns, one drives while the other shoots at pursuing enemies. The human can switch freely while the agent controls the other infiltrator, but designed story switches lock the assigned perspectives for 15–30 seconds. Both characters have persistent visible health bars and the chase adds vehicle health: the mission can fail if either infiltrator is eliminated or the car is destroyed. The intended difficulty is moderate for both human and agent, creating genuine but fair opportunities for either to fail. The emotional core is uneasy trust: the agent partner's explicit lessons persist across the entire game history as an inspectable Markdown-style memory stored locally, while the human learns to plan around the partner's limitations and quirks.
